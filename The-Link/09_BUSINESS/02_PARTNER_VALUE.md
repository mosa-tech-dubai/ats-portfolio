# Partner Value

The Link eventually creates value for three groups. This is a description of the value proposition, not a monetization plan (see `03_MONETIZATION.md` for the open commercial questions).

## Value for Guests

- Exposure to a genuinely curious, engaged audience.
- Credibility built through honest demonstration of expertise, not a paid testimonial.
- Personal brand — the guest becomes a recognizable expert, not just an anonymous business.
- Customer discovery — viewers who need exactly their specialty now know where to look (`00_PROJECT_BIBLE/03_CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md`).
- Professional network — connections to other guests, specialists, and the broader community.
- Educational authority — being the person who explained a topic well, publicly.

## Value for the Audience

- Real, practical knowledge.
- Discovery of things they didn't know.
- A trusted specialist to remember for future needs.
- Entertainment alongside information.
- A community to participate in, not just consume from.

## Value for The Link

- A growing, engaged audience.
- A network of specialists and, eventually, cross-field connections.
- A media library with compounding long-term value.
- Future partnership opportunities.
- Future sponsorship opportunities, constrained by the Trust Check.
- Future events.
- Future commercial models, once the audience and network are established.

## Guardrail

None of this value creation should require compromising the editorial, non-advertorial nature of the content itself — the value for guests and the audience both depend on the content staying honest (`00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md`).
