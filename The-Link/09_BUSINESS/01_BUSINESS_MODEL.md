# Business Model

No specific business model has been decided. This file documents the direction and constraints, not a locked plan.

## The Value Being Built

The Link creates three kinds of value that a future business model can be built on top of, per `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md` and `02_PARTNER_VALUE.md`:

- **Audience** — a real, engaged viewer base.
- **Network** — relationships with specialists, guests, and eventually communities across expertise fields.
- **Media library** — a growing, reusable catalogue of long-form and short-form content with lasting value beyond its initial publish date.

Any business model should monetize these assets without compromising them — see the Trust Check in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md`.

## What Is Explicitly Not Decided Yet

- Whether/when to introduce advertising, sponsorship, subscriptions, events, or other revenue streams.
- Company structure, if any, versus a personal/creator-led project.
- Investment or funding approach, if any.

## Constraint

Any monetization must remain compatible with audience trust (`09_BUSINESS/03_MONETIZATION.md`). This is a hard constraint on the business model, not a soft preference.

## Next Step

Revisit once the pilot and initial episode run validate the format and give real audience/engagement data to build a model around, rather than speculating without evidence.
