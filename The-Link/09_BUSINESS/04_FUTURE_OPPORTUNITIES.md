# Future Opportunities

Documented as future possibilities per `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md`. None of these are scheduled, committed, or currently in progress — they exist so the long-term direction isn't lost, and so near-term decisions (naming, brand, format) are made with these possibilities in mind rather than accidentally closing them off.

## Possible Future Directions

- **Expert directories** — a real, browsable answer to "who does this well?" built from the growing guest network.
- **Community events** — in-person or virtual gatherings for the audience and guest network.
- **Live sessions** — see `07_COMMUNITY/03_LIVE_FORMAT.md` for the nearer-term version of this.
- **Partnerships** — see `08_GROWTH/04_PARTNERSHIPS.md`.
- **Workshops** — educational sessions building on the expertise documented across episodes.
- **Educational content** — standalone educational products beyond the core episode/clip format.
- **Expert collaborations** — cross-field conversations between specialists, as noted in `08_GROWTH/03_COLLABORATIONS.md`.
- **Business networking** — enabling connections between specialists and between specialists and opportunities, extending the "connect people to opportunities" part of the vision.
- **Sponsored content** — only ever compatible with the Trust Check in `09_BUSINESS/03_MONETIZATION.md`.
- **Original productions** — new formats beyond the core interview/documentary format.
- **Other media formats** — once the core format is proven across the pilot and initial episodes.

## How to Use This Document

Treat this as a idea reservoir to revisit periodically (e.g., alongside pilot reviews or major planning checkpoints), not a roadmap with deadlines. Adding a new future idea here is always safe; moving something from here into an active workstream should go through the same Decision Principles (`00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md`) as any other strategic decision.
