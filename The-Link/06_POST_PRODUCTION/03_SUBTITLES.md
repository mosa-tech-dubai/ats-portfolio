# Subtitles

## Why Subtitles Are Standard, Not Optional

Most short-form viewing happens sound-off, and subtitles meaningfully help accessibility and retention on long-form content too. Every published piece of content should carry accurate captions.

## Principles

- Captions must accurately reflect what was said — no paraphrasing for brevity that changes meaning, especially on expertise/advice content where precision matters.
- Keep caption styling legible at small sizes (a real constraint on vertical short-form) — final styling spec depends on the visual identity system.
- Highlight or emphasize the key word/phrase in a line when it helps a fast-scrolling viewer grasp the hook immediately, without turning every caption into aggressive animated text.
