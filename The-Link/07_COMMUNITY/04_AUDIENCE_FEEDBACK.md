# Audience Feedback

## Why This Matters

Community strategy (`01_COMMUNITY_STRATEGY.md`) only works if feedback actually flows back into decisions — guest selection, question banks, format adjustments — rather than being collected and ignored.

## Feedback Channels to Monitor

- Comments on published episodes and clips (see `02_COMMENT_STRATEGY.md` for how these are cultivated)
- Retention and drop-off data per platform, read against the Episode Structure sections (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`) to see where viewers lose interest
- Direct messages/suggestions across platforms
- Reactions to community prompts (Question of the Episode, Who Should We Visit Next?, etc.)

## What to Do With Feedback

- Recurring guest suggestions → feed guest sourcing in `05_PRODUCTION/01_PRE_PRODUCTION.md`.
- Recurring viewer questions → feed the Question Bank (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/03_QUESTION_BANK.md`) and future live topics (`03_LIVE_FORMAT.md`).
- Consistent drop-off at a specific structural point → revisit that section of the Episode Structure and flag it for the next pilot-style review.
- Signals about brand tone, naming, or visual identity reception → feed directly into `04_PILOT/06_PILOT_REVIEW.md` and `01_BRAND/`.

## Status

Principles are approved; a formal cadence for reviewing feedback (weekly/monthly) and a specific tracking method (spreadsheet, tool) are `TO BE DEVELOPED` — to be set up once there's enough audience activity to warrant a system rather than ad hoc review.
