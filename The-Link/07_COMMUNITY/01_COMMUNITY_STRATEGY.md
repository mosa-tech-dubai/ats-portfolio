# Community Strategy

## The Goal Is Bigger Than Audience Growth

The ultimate goal is not view count — it's a community that participates: suggesting guests, asking questions, showing up for live sessions, and eventually forming a real network connecting people to people, expertise to people, and people to opportunities (`00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md`).

## Every Episode Should Invite Participation

Every episode ends with a community question designed to generate comments, not just "like and subscribe" (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`, Section J). See `02_COMMENT_STRATEGY.md` for how comments are actually cultivated and used.

## Recurring Community Mechanisms

- **Question of the Episode** — the community question from each episode, tracked and revisited.
- **Best Comment** — surfacing a standout audience comment (insight, story, or question) publicly.
- **Viewer Question** — audience questions folded into a future episode or live session.
- **Audience Choice** — letting the community weigh in on a decision (e.g., which of two guest candidates to visit next).
- **Who Should We Visit Next?** — a standing, recurring prompt across episodes.
- **What Should We Ask?** — banking audience questions ahead of a guest's return or a live session.
- **Expert Challenge** — a recurring format inviting the community (or another expert) to respond to a claim or demonstration.

## The Rhythm: Recorded Episodes → Accumulated Questions → Live Episode

A possible future rhythm: several recorded episodes accumulate community questions, then a live episode with a guest incorporates real-time audience interaction. **Not every episode should be live** — live is a periodic event layered on top of the core recorded format, not a replacement for it. Full detail: `03_LIVE_FORMAT.md`.

## Community as the Long-Term Network

As the project matures, community participation is the foundation for the longer-term vision in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md` — expert directories, events, workshops, and business networking all depend on an actual engaged community existing first. Don't skip building this to chase views.
