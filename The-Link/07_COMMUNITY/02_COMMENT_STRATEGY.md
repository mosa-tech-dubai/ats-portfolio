# Comment Strategy

## Designing for Comments, Not Just Views

The community question at the end of every episode (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`, Section J) exists specifically to generate conversation. Good community questions:

- Are genuinely easy to answer in one sentence (low friction).
- Invite personal experience ("What's the worst problem you've ever had with your car?") or opinion/participation ("What specialist should we visit next?") rather than yes/no answers.
- Are restated as text in the video description so people scrolling before watching can respond immediately.

## Responding to Comments

- Founder/team engagement with comments (replies, pinning strong ones, surfacing a "Best Comment") is part of the format, not optional community management overhead — it's what turns an audience into a community per `01_COMMUNITY_STRATEGY.md`.
- Prioritize responding to comments that contain real stories, questions, or specialist suggestions — these are directly useful for sourcing future guests and live-session topics.
- Never let a comment thread turn into an implicit advertisement war between competing businesses; moderate for that if it happens, gently redirecting toward the actual topic.

## Turning Comments Into Content

- Recurring viewer questions become fuel for "Viewer Question" segments or full episodes.
- Guest suggestions from comments feed the "Who Should We Visit Next?" mechanism.
- Banked questions across several episodes become the question set for a future live session (`03_LIVE_FORMAT.md`).
