# Platform Strategy

## Principle

Distribute across relevant platforms without blindly publishing identical content everywhere. One brand voice (`01_BRAND/04_TONE_OF_VOICE.md`), adapted per platform.

## Platforms in Scope

- **YouTube** — home of the full long-form episode and the primary Shorts destination. The archival, searchable home of the media library.
- **Instagram** — Reels for short-form extraction, Stories for behind-the-scenes and community prompts, feed posts for quote graphics/stills.
- **TikTok** — short-form extraction tuned to TikTok's faster, more native-feeling pacing and sound conventions.
- **Facebook** — long-form and short-form re-share, strong for community/group engagement around automotive audiences specifically.
- **LinkedIn** — selective use for business/entrepreneurship-angle stories and partnership visibility, not a primary content channel at launch.
- Additional platforms to be added as the project evolves; do not assume a platform belongs in rotation just because it's popular — evaluate it against the audience in `02_AUDIENCE.md`.

## Adaptation Principle, Not Duplication

Per platform, adapt:

- **Aspect ratio and cut length** (vertical short-form vs. horizontal long-form).
- **Hook style** — first 1–3 seconds must earn attention differently on TikTok/Reels than a YouTube thumbnail+title does.
- **Caption and on-screen text density** — higher for sound-off viewing environments (feed-based platforms) than for YouTube.
- **Native conventions** — trending audio/format norms on TikTok/Reels vs. more editorial framing on YouTube/LinkedIn.

What does **not** change per platform: the tone, the honesty of the content, and the indirect-marketing rule.
