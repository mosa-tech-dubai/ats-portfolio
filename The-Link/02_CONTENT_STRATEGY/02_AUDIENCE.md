# Audience

## Primary Audience (Launch Phase — Automotive)

- Car owners and enthusiasts who are curious about how their vehicles actually work.
- People currently dealing with, or worried about, a specific car problem, who arrive via search/social because an episode or clip addresses that exact problem.
- Enthusiast communities around specific marques (BMW, Mercedes, classic/collector cars) with strong existing online engagement habits (forums, groups, comment culture).
- General curious viewers who enjoy documentary-style, "behind the scenes of a skill" content, independent of car ownership.

## What This Audience Wants

- Real, practical knowledge they can use or reference later.
- A trustworthy specialist to associate with a specific problem, without being sold to.
- Entertainment and personality, not just facts.
- A reason to comment, argue, share a story, or suggest the next guest — see `07_COMMUNITY/`.

## The Two-Sided Audience Design

The Link is deliberately built to satisfy two audience states at once:

1. **Problem-driven viewers** — arrive because they have a BMW issue, a specific question, or a search intent. They discover a specialist naturally through the content and now know where to look if they ever need that expertise. This is the indirect-marketing mechanism described in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/03_CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md`.
2. **Curiosity-driven viewers** — arrive with no specific need, just enjoying discovery-style content about how things work and who does them well. This audience is what allows the brand to expand beyond automotive, since their interest is in the format, not the vertical.

Both audiences must be served by the same episode. Content that only works for one (e.g., overly technical diagnostics with no story, or pure entertainment with no real information) is off-brief.

## What We Are Explicitly Not Targeting

- Audiences looking for pure comparison/review/ranking content ("best BMW shop in [city]") — that is a different, lower-trust content category The Link does not compete in.
- Audiences looking for scripted entertainment with no real informational payoff.
