# Content Philosophy

## The Governing Idea

Capture one rich experience and turn it into an entire content ecosystem. The goal is never "make one podcast episode" — it's to extract maximum honest value from one real conversation in one real place.

This document sets the philosophy; the mechanics of how one shoot becomes many pieces of content live in `05_CONTENT_REPURPOSING.md`.

## What Makes Content "The Link" Content

Every piece — long-form or six seconds — should be checkable against `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/03_CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md`'s formula: **Experience + Story + Discovery + Connection + Value.** A clip that has none of these is filler, not content.

## Value Before Views, Not Instead Of Views

Value is the priority, but it does not excuse ignoring craft. Content is still built deliberately for attention, retention, and shareability — a true, useful moment that nobody watches helps no one. Structure, pacing, and hooks matter exactly as much as accuracy and honesty.

## Editorial, Not Advertorial

Content stays editorial-first. Guest and business information (name, location, links, contact) lives in descriptions and end cards — never inside the narrative voice of the piece itself. See `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md`, the Ad Test.

## Built for Extraction From the Start

Because one shoot must generate many pieces, content is planned with extraction in mind *before* filming, not discovered afterward in the edit. This shapes the shot list, the highlight-spotting habit during filming (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md`), and the interview approach (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`).

## One Voice, Many Formats

The tone from `01_BRAND/04_TONE_OF_VOICE.md` stays constant across every platform and every format length. What changes is pacing, framing, and length — not personality. See `04_PLATFORM_STRATEGY.md`.
