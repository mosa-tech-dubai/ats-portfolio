# Content Pillars

Every episode and its extracted clips should draw from these recurring pillars. Not every pillar needs to appear in every single piece of content, but a healthy episode touches most of them, and a healthy publishing calendar rotates across all of them. These pillars map directly to the Episode Structure sections in `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`.

## 1. Origin & Person

Who this person is and how they became good at what they do. Human-interest core of the brand.

## 2. The Place

The workshop, garage, studio, or facility as a character in its own right — tools, environment, atmosphere.

## 3. Expertise & Value

The educational core: common problems, mistakes, misconceptions, expensive errors, warning signs, maintenance advice, specialist-only knowledge.

## 4. Real Stories

Unusual cases, funny situations, difficult jobs, memorable customers, failures and successes. This is where personality and rewatch value live.

## 5. Demonstration

Expertise shown, not just described — a real diagnosis, repair, or object explained hands-on wherever access and permission allow.

## 6. Human / Light Moments

Unscripted humor, personality, spontaneity — never forced.

## 7. Practical Takeaway

The one clear, useful thing a viewer walks away able to act on.

## 8. Community Prompt

A question posed to the audience designed to generate real comments and participation, not just "like and subscribe." See `07_COMMUNITY/02_COMMENT_STRATEGY.md`.

## How Pillars Map to Short-Form Content

Each pillar tends to produce a different short-form content type (see `05_CONTENT_REPURPOSING.md` for the full breakdown):

| Pillar | Typical short-form output |
|---|---|
| Origin & Person | Origin-story clip, "how I got into this" reel |
| The Place | Behind-the-scenes / tour-style short |
| Expertise & Value | "Did you know?" clip, myth vs. fact clip |
| Real Stories | Funny clip, "you won't believe this" clip |
| Demonstration | Problem/solution clip, diagnostic clip |
| Human / Light Moments | Funny clip, personality clip |
| Practical Takeaway | Expert quote card, practical tip clip |
| Community Prompt | Teaser clip, comment-bait clip |
