# Team Roles

No team roles beyond the founder have been defined. This project currently assumes a small or solo operator handling sourcing, interviewing, filming, editing, and publishing, per the assumption noted in `05_PRODUCTION/01_PRE_PRODUCTION.md`.

## Roles Likely Needed as the Project Scales

Documented as anticipated future roles, not current hires or commitments:

- **Interviewer/Host** — conducts the on-camera conversation, following `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`.
- **Producer** — guest sourcing and vetting, logistics, `05_PRODUCTION/01_PRE_PRODUCTION.md`.
- **Camera/Audio operator(s)** — filming execution, `05_PRODUCTION/02_FILMING.md`, `03_AUDIO.md`, `04_CAMERA.md`.
- **Highlight logger** — a dedicated role during filming per `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md`, distinct from the interviewer.
- **Editor (long-form)** — `06_POST_PRODUCTION/01_EDITING_GUIDELINES.md`.
- **Editor (short-form)** — `06_POST_PRODUCTION/02_SHORT_FORM_EDITING.md`, potentially a distinct skill set from long-form editing.
- **Community manager** — `07_COMMUNITY/02_COMMENT_STRATEGY.md`, `04_AUDIENCE_FEEDBACK.md`.

## Next Step
