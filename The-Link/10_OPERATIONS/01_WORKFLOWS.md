# Workflows

## End-to-End Episode Workflow

1. **Source and vet a guest** — `05_PRODUCTION/01_PRE_PRODUCTION.md`
2. **Plan the shoot** — question plan, shot list, logistics — `01_PRE_PRODUCTION.md`, `04_PILOT/04_PILOT_SHOT_LIST.md`
3. **Film** — `05_PRODUCTION/02_FILMING.md`, maintaining a live highlight log — `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md`
4. **Back up footage** immediately — `05_PRODUCTION/06_PRODUCTION_CHECKLIST.md`
5. **Edit the long-form cut** — `06_POST_PRODUCTION/01_EDITING_GUIDELINES.md`
6. **Extract short-form content** from the highlight log — `06_POST_PRODUCTION/02_SHORT_FORM_EDITING.md`
7. **Prepare captions, thumbnails, and delivery assets** — `06_POST_PRODUCTION/03_SUBTITLES.md`, `04_THUMBNAILS.md`, `05_DELIVERY.md`
8. **Schedule and publish** across platforms per `02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/04_PLATFORM_STRATEGY.md`, staggering teaser → full episode → remaining short-form
9. **Engage the community** post-publish — `07_COMMUNITY/02_COMMENT_STRATEGY.md`
10. **Review performance and feedback**, feeding learnings back into guest sourcing, the question bank, and format adjustments — `07_COMMUNITY/04_AUDIENCE_FEEDBACK.md`
