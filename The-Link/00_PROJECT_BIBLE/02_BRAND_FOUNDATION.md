# Brand Foundation (Condensed)

This is the condensed brand summary for quick reference inside the Project Bible. Full depth, rationale, and all working documents live in `01_BRAND/`.

## Working Name

**The Link** — chosen because it represents the central idea of the whole project: connecting people to people, people to expertise, hidden experts to audiences, questions to answers, and eventually customers to the specialists who can actually help them.

The name must not depend on cars and must still make sense years from now once the project expands to other fields. See `01_BRAND/01_WORKING_NAME.md`.

## Documented Alternatives (do not delete)

1. The Link *(current preferred working name)*
2. Uncovered
3. Insight
4. Hidden Experts
5. Beyond

The final naming decision remains open. See `01_BRAND/02_NAME_ALTERNATIVES.md`.

## Positioning

The Link sits at the intersection of documentary curiosity, practical education, and community-built trust. It is not a review channel, not a business directory, not a traditional interview podcast. It positions itself as the show that goes where expertise actually lives and brings back what's true. Full detail in `01_BRAND/03_BRAND_POSITIONING.md`.

## Tone

Curious, intelligent, friendly, slightly comedic, human, practical, professional without being corporate, entertaining without becoming fake, informative without becoming a lecture. Full detail in `01_BRAND/04_TONE_OF_VOICE.md`.

## Visual Identity

Not yet finalized. Logo, typography, color, motion identity, thumbnails, and camera language are all open. `STATUS: TO BE DEVELOPED` — see `01_BRAND/06_VISUAL_IDENTITY.md`. ## Naming Convention for Extensions

Possible future sub-brands (not committed, not scheduled): The Link Podcast, The Link Live, The Link Shorts, The Link Community, The Link Events. These exist as naming space to grow into, not a locked roadmap.
