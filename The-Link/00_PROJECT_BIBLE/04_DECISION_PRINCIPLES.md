# Decision Principles

A working set of tests for evaluating whether a new idea, episode concept, guest, script, or marketing angle actually fits The Link. Use these before producing creative work, not after.

## The Ad Test

> "Does this feel like the viewer is being told where to go, or does it feel like the viewer discovered where to go?"

If a script, caption, or clip says or implies "go to this business," rewrite it. The information (name, location, contact) belongs in the description and end card — never in the narrative voice of the piece. See `03_CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md`.

## The Value Test

> "What did I learn that I didn't know before this?"

If nobody on the team can answer this in one sentence about a piece of content, it isn't ready to publish.

## The Portability Test

> "Would this structure, question, or format still work if the guest were a watchmaker instead of a mechanic?"

If the answer is no because the idea is inherently car-specific (not just car-flavored), be cautious — it may be building brand equity that doesn't transfer when the project expands beyond automotive.

## The Human Test

> "Does this reveal a person, or does it showcase a service?"

Expertise questions should always be in service of understanding the person and their craft, not cataloguing their services.

## The Story-First Rule

> "If the guest is telling a good story, do not interrupt to get back to the question list."

Prepared questions exist to unlock stories, not to be completed as a checklist. See `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`.

## The Scalability Check

> "Could this decision box the brand in permanently?"

Applies to naming, visual identity, platform commitments, and content formats. Prefer decisions that are strong now and still make sense in three years and five verticals, over decisions that are only strong for automotive content today.

## The Trust Check

> "Would this erode the audience's trust in why we're really there?"

Applies especially to monetization, sponsorships, and partnerships (see `09_BUSINESS/03_MONETIZATION.md`). Any commercial model must survive this check before being adopted.

## When in Doubt

If a creative or strategic decision seems to conflict with anything in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/`, don't quietly resolve it. Flag the conflict explicitly, explain the tension, and let a human make the call. This applies equally to AI assistants and human collaborators.
