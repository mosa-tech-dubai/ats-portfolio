# READ ME FIRST — The Project Bible

This document is the highest-level context for The Link. Read this before doing any strategic or creative work on the project — writing scripts, designing brand assets, planning episodes, building marketing, or making structural decisions.

**Rule for any AI assistant (Claude Code, ChatGPT, or other tools) working in this project:** Read this file, and the rest of `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/`, before generating strategic or creative output. Never invent a new direction that contradicts the Project Bible without explicitly flagging the conflict to the human operator first. If a request seems to contradict a decision recorded here, say so, name the conflict, and ask before proceeding.

---

## What The Link Is

The Link is a media platform and community built around one idea: **connect people with real-world expertise.**

We go to where expertise actually lives — garages, workshops, studios, dealerships, specialist facilities — sit with the person in their real environment, and uncover who they are, what they do, how they got good at it, and what they know that most people never get to hear.

It is a discovery experience for the audience and a recognition experience for the guest. See `03_CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md` for the full articulation of this.

## What The Link Is Not

- It is not "simply a podcast." Long-form conversation is one output, not the product.
- It is not an advertising vehicle. Episodes must never feel like "go visit this garage, they're the best."
- It is not a car channel. Automobiles are the starting niche, not the ceiling. See `01_PROJECT_VISION.md`.
- It is not corporate, scripted, or lecture-like in tone.
- It is not committed to a rigid interview script, a fixed visual identity, or a locked brand name. Several foundational elements are intentionally still open — see "Unresolved Decisions" below.

## Core Philosophy (short version)

**Experience + Story + Discovery + Connection + Value.**

We don't primarily ask "what service do you sell?" We ask "what have you learned from doing this for years?" Full detail in `03_CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md`.

## Current Working Brand

**The Link.** Chosen because it represents the central idea of connecting people to people, people to expertise, hidden experts to audiences, and questions to answers. The name is not finalized — four alternatives remain actively documented and available: *Uncovered, Insight, Hidden Experts, Beyond*. See `01_BRAND/01_WORKING_NAME.md` and `01_BRAND/02_NAME_ALTERNATIVES.md`.

## The Audience

People who are curious about how things really work and who wants real, practical knowledge from people who actually hold it — starting with car owners and enthusiasts, expanding as the format expands. Full detail in `02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/02_AUDIENCE.md`.

## The Content Model

One filmed session is treated as a content mine, not a single deliverable. A single shoot should be capable of producing a full episode plus a wide range of short-form and supporting content (Shorts, Reels, clips, quote graphics, teasers, etc.). Full detail in `02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/05_CONTENT_REPURPOSING.md`.

## Episode Philosophy

Episodes happen primarily at the guest's actual place of work. The location is part of the storytelling — the audience should feel taken somewhere they normally couldn't access. Full detail in `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`.

## Long-Term Vision

The ambition is not "build a successful podcast." It is to build a media and community network that discovers, documents, and connects real-world expertise — a place people go to answer "who actually knows how to do this?" Full detail in `01_PROJECT_VISION.md`.

## Current Decisions (DECIDED)

See `10_OPERATIONS/` and `PROJECT_STATUS.md` (project root) for the full living list. The headline decisions:

- The project is bigger than a podcast.
- The project is about real-world expertise and connection, not service advertising.
- The initial content focus is automotive, used as a proving ground, not a ceiling.
- The format happens primarily at the guest's real location.
- Content must be useful and entertaining — value before views.
- Marketing is indirect and content-first, never a direct pitch for the guest's business.
- Community participation is a core mechanic, not an afterthought.
- Long-form content is designed from the outset to generate short-form content.
- Live interaction is a planned future layer of the ecosystem, not a launch requirement.
- The brand must scale beyond automotive into other expertise fields.
- The working brand name is "The Link"; alternatives remain documented and open.
- The pilot is a test lab for the whole system, not simply "Episode 1."
