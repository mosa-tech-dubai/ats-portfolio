# Collaborations

No formal collaboration strategy exists yet. This file holds the space and constraints for that decision.

## Types of Collaboration to Eventually Consider

- **Cross-expert collaborations** — e.g., a mechanical specialist and an automotive engineer discussing the same problem from two angles, mentioned as a long-term possibility in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md`.
- **Guest-to-guest referrals** — a specialist recommending another specialist worth featuring, a natural extension of the "Who Should We Visit Next?" mechanism (`07_COMMUNITY/01_COMMUNITY_STRATEGY.md`).
- **Other creators/channels** — collaborations with creators in adjacent spaces (automotive content, craftsmanship content) as the audience grows.

## Constraint Any Collaboration Must Satisfy

Must pass the same tests as any other content: the Ad Test, the Value Test, and the Trust Check in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md`. A collaboration that exists mainly to cross-promote, without genuine expertise/story/discovery value, is off-brief.

Revisit after the pilot and initial episodes establish a real content rhythm — collaborations are easier to evaluate once there's a working format to collaborate within.
