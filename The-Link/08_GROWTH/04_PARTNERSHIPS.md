No formal partnership strategy exists yet. This file holds the space and constraints for that decision. See also `09_BUSINESS/02_PARTNER_VALUE.md` and `09_BUSINESS/04_FUTURE_OPPORTUNITIES.md` for the related business-side documentation.

## Types of Partnership to Eventually Consider

- Specialist/business partnerships beyond a one-off episode (e.g., an ongoing relationship with a recurring guest or facility).
- Platform or distribution partnerships.
- Sponsorship arrangements — see `09_BUSINESS/03_MONETIZATION.md` for the trust constraints any sponsorship must satisfy before being considered.
- Community/event partnerships supporting the long-term vision of live sessions and expert directories (`00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md`).

## Constraint Any Partnership Must Satisfy

Must survive the Trust Check in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md`: would this partnership erode the audience's trust in why The Link is really there? Any partnership that fails this test should not be pursued regardless of commercial upside.
