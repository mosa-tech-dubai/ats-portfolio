# Discovery

## How New Viewers Find The Link

- **Problem-driven search/social discovery** — a viewer with a specific issue (e.g., a BMW electrical fault) finds a relevant clip or episode addressing exactly that problem. This is the primary mechanism by which automotive content proves the broader concept (`02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/02_AUDIENCE.md`).
- **Short-form algorithmic discovery** — Shorts/Reels/TikToks surface to viewers with no prior awareness of the brand, using strong hooks built from real highlights (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md`, `06_POST_PRODUCTION/02_SHORT_FORM_EDITING.md`).
- **Enthusiast community discovery** — marque-specific forums, groups, and comment sections where car enthusiasts already gather and share content organically.
- **Guest's own network** — a specialist sharing their own episode with their existing customers and followers, which also demonstrates the indirect-marketing value proposition to future guests (`09_BUSINESS/02_PARTNER_VALUE.md`).
- **Cross-episode discovery** — a viewer who found one episode explores others via the "Who Should We Visit Next?" and related-content mechanisms (`07_COMMUNITY/01_COMMUNITY_STRATEGY.md`).

## SEO / Search Considerations

- Titles and descriptions should reflect real, specific problems and topics covered (e.g., a specific fault or misconception), since this is how problem-driven viewers actually search — without resorting to clickbait that fails the Value Test.
- Chapters/timestamps in long-form descriptions help both search discoverability and viewer navigation to the part they need.
