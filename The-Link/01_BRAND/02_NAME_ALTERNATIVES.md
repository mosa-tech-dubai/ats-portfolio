# Name Alternatives

These five names were specifically identified during brand development. All five must remain documented. **Do not delete any of them, even after a final name is chosen** — keep this file as a historical record and fallback option.

## 1. The Link — *current preferred working name*

Represents connection directly. Vertical-agnostic. See `01_WORKING_NAME.md` for full rationale.

## 2. Uncovered

Leans into the discovery angle — the feeling of finding something that was hidden in plain sight. Strong fit for the "audience discovers what they didn't know" half of the brand promise. Weaker fit for the "connection" half — doesn't inherently suggest community or networking.

## 3. Insight

Leans into the educational/value angle — practical knowledge, expert perspective. Clean and scalable across verticals. Risk: fairly generic word, used by many other media and business-consulting brands, which could make trademark and search discoverability harder.

## 4. Hidden Experts

Very literal and immediately understandable — tells the viewer exactly what the show is about. Strong initial-hook clarity. Risk: locks the brand narrative around "hidden," which may feel dated once a guest is well known, and reads slightly more listicle/clickbait than documentary.

## 5. Beyond

Abstract, evocative, aspirational; would require more brand-building work to convey what the show actually is, since it doesn't describe the mechanic (connection) or the content (expertise) on its own. Strongest "years from now" flexibility of all five, but weakest immediate clarity.

## How to Use This List

- Do not treat "The Link" as permanently locked. Revisit this list at the pilot review stage (`04_PILOT/06_PILOT_REVIEW.md`) and any time before meaningful visual identity or legal investment.
- If a name change is ever considered, re-run each candidate through `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md` (especially the Scalability Check) before deciding.
- New candidate names discovered later should be added to this list, not used to silently replace it.
