# Taglines

No tagline has been chosen or approved. This file exists to hold the space and the constraints for that decision — not to pretend a decision has been made.

## Constraints Any Tagline Must Satisfy

Derived from `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/` and `03_BRAND_POSITIONING.md`:

- Must not mention cars, garages, or any single vertical.
- Must capture *connection* and/or *discovery of real expertise* — the two halves of the brand promise.
- Must survive the Scalability Check in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md` (still true in five verticals).
- Should be usable as a spoken sign-off line as well as written copy (end cards, channel description, merchandise).

## Directions to Explore (unreviewed, for brainstorming only)

These are starting directions, not candidates — none has been evaluated or approved:

- Language around "real expertise, real people"
- Language around "the people who actually know"
- Language around "connection" as the verb, not just the brand name
- Language around discovery ("what you didn't know you needed to know")
