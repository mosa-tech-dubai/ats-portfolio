# Video Prompts

Prompt patterns for short-form video concepts, editing direction, and content extraction planning. Read `00_AI_READ_FIRST.md` first.

## Short-Form Extraction Planning

> "Given this highlight log from a shoot: [paste log]. Using `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md` and `06_POST_PRODUCTION/02_SHORT_FORM_EDITING.md`, map each highlight to a clip type and suggested platform (`02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/04_PLATFORM_STRATEGY.md`). Prioritize which 3–5 clips to cut first."

## Short-Form Hook Writing

> "Write 3 hook variants (first 1–3 seconds of on-screen text/spoken line) for a clip about [highlight/topic], following the short-form principles in `06_POST_PRODUCTION/02_SHORT_FORM_EDITING.md`. Do not imply a stronger or different claim than what the guest actually said."

## Teaser Clip Concept

> "Using the strongest unused highlight from this episode: [paste highlight], draft a teaser clip concept to release ahead of the full episode, per `02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/05_CONTENT_REPURPOSING.md`."

## Editing Direction Notes

> "Given this rough cut description or footage log: [paste], suggest edit structure following `06_POST_PRODUCTION/01_EDITING_GUIDELINES.md` — where to trim, where to preserve genuine reaction/humor, and where the cold open candidate likely sits."

## Guardrail for All Video Work

Never suggest a cut, caption, or hook that changes the meaning of what a guest said, even if it would perform better — flag the tradeoff instead of silently choosing the punchier but less accurate version.
