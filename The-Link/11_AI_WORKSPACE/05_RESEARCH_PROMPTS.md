# Research Prompts

Prompt patterns for guest research, topic research, and background prep. Read `00_AI_READ_FIRST.md` first.

## Guest / Specialty Background Research

> "Research common problems, misconceptions, and terminology for [specialty, e.g., BMW transmission diagnostics] so the interviewer can ask informed questions without needing to prove expertise on camera, per `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`. Summarize in plain language a non-specialist interviewer can actually use."

## Question Bank Expansion

> "Given this specialty: [X], suggest additional questions to add to `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/03_QUESTION_BANK.md` under the existing categories (Intro, Expertise, Story, Educational, Personal, Demonstration, Community). Match the existing question style — open, curious, not leading."

## Vertical Expansion Research

> "Research how the Episode Structure (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`) and Question Bank would need to adapt for a [new field, e.g., watchmaking / aviation] specialist, per the expansion direction in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md`. Flag anything that seems automotive-specific rather than genuinely portable."

## Competitive / Landscape Research

> "Research existing content in [space] and compare it against The Link's positioning in `01_BRAND/03_BRAND_POSITIONING.md`. Identify where existing content is advertorial or review-based versus where it achieves genuine documentary/educational value."

## Guardrail for All Research Work

Research should inform better questions and context, not be used to fabricate confident specialist claims The Link would then present as its own expertise — the guest is the expert; the research is only to help ask better questions.
