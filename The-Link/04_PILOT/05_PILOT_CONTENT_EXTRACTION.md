# Pilot Content Extraction Plan

## Purpose

Test the content repurposing system (`02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/05_CONTENT_REPURPOSING.md`) on a real shoot and measure what actually comes out of one recording session.

## Target Outputs to Attempt From the Pilot

- [ ] Full long-form episode
- [ ] YouTube upload, optimized (title, description, chapters, end card)
- [ ] At least 3–5 Shorts/Reels/TikToks pulled from logged highlights
- [ ] At least 1 educational clip ("Did you know?" or myth vs. fact style)
- [ ] At least 1 funny/personality clip
- [ ] At least 1 problem/solution clip
- [ ] At least 1 expert quote graphic
- [ ] At least 1 teaser clip, released ahead of the full episode
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes content (short, informal)
- [ ] A set of still images usable for thumbnails and social posts

## What to Measure

- How many usable outputs the raw footage actually supported, versus the target list above.
- How much editor time each output category took, to inform realistic future scheduling (`10_OPERATIONS/01_WORKFLOWS.md`).
- Which highlight categories from `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md` were easiest/hardest to turn into finished clips.
- Whether the live highlight log during filming actually sped up post-production, or whether editors still had to rewatch everything.

## Feed Findings Into

- `06_PILOT_REVIEW.md` — the formal analysis.
- `06_POST_PRODUCTION/02_SHORT_FORM_EDITING.md` — refine the short-form process based on what worked.
- `10_OPERATIONS/01_WORKFLOWS.md` — build a realistic production-to-publish timeline based on actual pilot data, not assumptions.
