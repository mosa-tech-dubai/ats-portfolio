# Pilot Shot List

This is a starting checklist for the pilot shoot, built from `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`. Exact camera/audio setup guidance lives in `05_PRODUCTION/04_CAMERA.md` and `05_PRODUCTION/03_AUDIO.md` — this document is about *what* to capture, not *how*.

## Interview Coverage

- [ ] Primary interview angle on guest (main talking-head coverage for the whole conversation)
- [ ] Secondary/reaction angle on interviewer or wide two-shot, for cutaways
- [ ] Room tone and ambient sound at the interview location

## The Place

- [ ] Wide establishing shot of the location (exterior if possible)
- [ ] Wide establishing shot of the interior/workspace
- [ ] Detail shots: tools, equipment, workbenches, signage
- [ ] Cars/objects currently being worked on
- [ ] People working in the background (with permission)
- [ ] Any unusual or visually interesting object the guest points out
- [ ] A walking shot following the guest through the space (this becomes the connective tissue between interview segments)

## Demonstration

- [ ] Close-up hands-on footage of the guest actually doing the work they described
- [ ] The specific problem/part/tool referenced in the expertise or story segments, shown, not just described
- [ ] Reaction shots during the demonstration

## Human Moments

- [ ] Candid, unscripted footage during setup/breaks — often where the best personality moments happen
- [ ] Any spontaneous humor as it occurs (do not wait to "set up" a joke shot)

## Cutaway / B-Roll Safety Net

- [ ] General B-roll of the location beyond what's scripted above, to give editors flexibility for pacing and short-form cuts
- [ ] Still photos throughout, for thumbnails and social stills (see `06_POST_PRODUCTION/04_THUMBNAILS.md`)

## Pilot-Specific Instrumentation

- [ ] Highlight log maintained live per `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md`
- [ ] Notes on what was hard to capture, or what this list missed, for the post-pilot review

## Status

This list is a first draft to be stress-tested by the actual pilot shoot. Expect to revise it in `06_PILOT_REVIEW.md` and fold corrections into `05_PRODUCTION/02_FILMING.md` once validated.
