# Pilot Question Plan

## Source

Pulled from `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/03_QUESTION_BANK.md`. This is the pilot's specific selection and sequencing plan — a working starting point, to be used exactly as the Interview Framework describes (a reservoir, not a script).

## Suggested Flow for a BMW Specialist Pilot

**Opening (Section C — Who Is This Person?)**
- Who are you, and what made you specialize in BMWs specifically?
- How did you start?
- How long have you been doing this?

**Moving Through the Shop (Section D — The Place)**
- What are we looking at right now? *(asked naturally per station/tool/car, not as a formal list)*
- Can you show us? *(demonstration questions, used opportunistically throughout)*

**Expertise Block (Section E)**
- What problem do you see most often on BMWs?
- What do customers misunderstand about their own car?
- What mistake costs BMW owners the most money?
- What do people replace unnecessarily?
- When should someone come to a specialist instead of a general shop?

**Story Block (Section F)**
- What's the strangest BMW case you've seen?
- What's the hardest job you've ever done on one?
- Have you ever solved something nobody else could figure out?
- What's the funniest thing that's happened here?

**Demonstration (Section G)**
- Walk through an actual diagnostic or repair in progress, if one is available and the customer/owner has given permission.

**Personal / Light Moment (Section C/H)**
- What are you most proud of?
- What keeps you interested in this after all these years?

**Closing (Sections I/J)**
- If you own a BMW, what's one thing you wish every owner knew?
- What should we ask you next time? / What specialist should we visit next?

## Pilot-Specific Notes

- Do not force every question above into the final interview — this plan exists so the interviewer walks in prepared, per the Interviewer Rule in `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`.
- After the pilot, record in `06_PILOT_REVIEW.md` which questions actually produced the best material, to sharpen future guest-specific question plans.
