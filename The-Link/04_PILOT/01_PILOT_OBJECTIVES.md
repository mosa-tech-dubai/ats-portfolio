# Pilot Objectives

## The Pilot Is Not "Episode 1"

The pilot is a test laboratory for the entire system described in this project. It should be treated as an experiment to learn from and analyze, not simply the first piece of content to publish. See `06_PILOT_REVIEW.md` for how findings get turned into decisions.

## What the Pilot Must Test

- Brand name (`01_BRAND/01_WORKING_NAME.md` and alternatives)
- Brand tone (`01_BRAND/04_TONE_OF_VOICE.md`)
- Episode structure (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`)
- Interview framework (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`)
- Filming style
- Movement/location storytelling
- Guest interaction
- Audio
- Camera setup
- Cold open (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/05_COLD_OPEN.md`)
- Intro
- Outro (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/06_OUTRO_AND_CTA.md`)
- Thumbnail
- Short-form extraction (`02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/05_CONTENT_REPURPOSING.md`)
- Audience reaction
- Comments
- Retention
- Workflow
- Editing process

## The Starting Example: A BMW Specialist Garage

A strong initial pilot candidate is a BMW specialist garage, because:

- BMW has a large, engaged enthusiast audience.
- There are recurring problems that generate genuinely useful content.
- Specialist garages have stories worth telling.
- Technical knowledge translates well into simple public education.
- The garage provides strong visual activity for filming.
- The episode can produce many short-form clips.

**The BMW episode must not become a BMW advertisement.** It is the environment for testing the larger The Link concept — the choice of guest matters far less than what the team learns from producing and releasing it. If a different guest with equally strong access and story potential becomes available before the pilot, they are an acceptable substitute; the BMW-specialist framing is an example, not a requirement.

## Success Criteria

The pilot succeeds if it produces clear, evidence-based answers (not guesses) to the open questions in `06_PILOT_REVIEW.md` — even if some of those answers are "this didn't work, change it." A pilot that goes smoothly but teaches nothing has failed its actual purpose.
