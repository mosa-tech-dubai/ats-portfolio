# Question Bank

This is a large question bank organized by category — not a fixed list that must always be asked in order. The interviewer should arrive prepared with these questions but listen actively and follow interesting answers, per `02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`.

## Intro Questions

- Who are you?
- What do you do?
- How did you start?
- Why this field?
- How long have you been doing it?
- What made you specialize?

## Expertise Questions

- What problem do you see most often?
- What do customers misunderstand?
- What mistake costs people the most money?
- What do people replace unnecessarily?
- What should customers check first?
- When should someone go to a specialist?

## Story Questions

- What's the strangest case you've seen?
- What's the hardest job you've ever done?
- What's a problem you thought would be simple but wasn't?
- Have you ever solved something nobody else could?
- What's the funniest thing that happened here?

## Educational Questions

- What is the most common misconception?
- What should every owner know?
- What warning sign should people never ignore?
- What advice would save people money?
- What's something professionals know that customers usually don't?

## Personal Questions

- What are you most proud of?
- What's something you learned the hard way?
- What would you do differently if starting again?
- Who influenced you?
- What keeps you interested?

## Demonstration Questions

- Can you show us?
- What are we looking at?
- What would normally go wrong here?
- How do you diagnose this?
- What would a normal customer notice?

## Community Questions

- What question do customers ask you constantly?
- What should we ask you next time?
- What specialist should we visit next?
- What's the biggest misconception you want to correct?

## Using This Bank

- Categories roughly map to Episode Structure sections in `01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`: Intro → B/C, Expertise → E, Story → F, Educational → E/I, Personal → C/H, Demonstration → G, Community → J.
- Add new questions to this bank after each shoot as good ones emerge in the field — this should grow over time, not stay static.
- Vertical-specific question variants (e.g., automotive-specific phrasing) can be added as a sub-section here once there's enough episode volume to justify it; don't fork this file per vertical prematurely.
