# Interview Framework

## The Core Rule

The interviewer must know when **not** to ask another question.

If the guest starts telling an interesting story, **let them talk.** The objective is to unlock the guest's expertise and personality, not to demonstrate the interviewer's own knowledge or complete a checklist of prepared questions.

## The Short Follow-Up Toolkit

When a guest is in a good story or explanation, use short, low-friction follow-ups instead of new questions:

- "How?"
- "Why?"
- "What happened next?"
- "Show us."
- "Wait, how did you figure that out?"
- "Tell me more about that."

These keep momentum without redirecting the guest away from what they're already saying.

## How Prepared Questions Should Be Used

The interviewer should arrive with prepared questions from `03_QUESTION_BANK.md`, organized by category, but treat them as a reservoir to draw from — not a sequence to complete. Good practice:

1. Know the categories cold (intro, expertise, story, educational, personal, demonstration, community) so the right follow-up is available at any moment.
2. Use prepared questions to open a new area or to recover momentum, never to interrupt a story in progress.
3. Actively listen for a throwaway line that's actually the best material in the interview ("oh yeah, that reminds me of the time...") and chase it immediately.

## Reading the Guest

- Nervous or reserved guests benefit from starting in "The Place" (walking and pointing at things) before deep interview questions — motion and physical objects lower guard faster than sitting and talking.
- Naturally talkative guests may need gentle redirection back toward a category once a story is fully told, using a bridge from the question bank rather than an abrupt topic change.
- Watch for a guest becoming guarded or salesy in tone — a sign the conversation has drifted toward pitching their business. Redirect toward experience and story ("what did you learn from that job," not "why should people choose you").

## Relationship to the Question Bank

This document describes *how* to interview. `03_QUESTION_BANK.md` provides *what* to ask, organized by category. Neither is a script — together they're a toolkit the interviewer draws from live, guided by the Interviewer Rule above.
