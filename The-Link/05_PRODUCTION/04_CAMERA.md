# Camera

Specific equipment, shot grammar, and multi-camera setup have not yet been finalized — the pilot shoot is explicitly designed to test camera setup in practice (see `04_PILOT/01_PILOT_OBJECTIVES.md`).

## Principles to Follow Until a Full SOP Exists

- Coverage should support both long-form editing (matching cuts, reaction shots) and short-form extraction (tight, vertical-croppable framing on key moments) — see `02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/05_CONTENT_REPURPOSING.md`.
- The location itself should be filmed as a character (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`, Section D) — plan for movement shots, not only static interview framing.
- Favor a small, mobile setup that can follow a guest naturally through a workspace over a heavy, fixed multi-camera rig, especially at this stage of the project.
- Shoot with vertical-format extraction in mind (leave headroom/framing that survives a crop to 9:16) so short-form clips don't require re-shooting or awkward reframing.

## Camera Language / Visual Identity

Longer-term shot grammar and "camera language" as a brand signature is tracked in `01_BRAND/06_VISUAL_IDENTITY.md` and should be developed together with that document, not independently.
