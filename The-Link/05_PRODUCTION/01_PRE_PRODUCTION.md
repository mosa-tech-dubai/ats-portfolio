# Pre-Production

## Guest Sourcing and Vetting

- Identify a candidate specialist whose expertise, location, and story potential fit `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/01_PROJECT_VISION.md` and the content pillars in `02_CONTENT_STRATEGY/03_CONTENT_PILLARS.md`.
- Confirm the guest has a real, visually interesting workplace — the location is part of the storytelling per `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`, Section D.
- A short pre-call with the guest to gauge willingness to talk openly (not just demo their work) is worth doing before committing a shoot day — reserved, guarded guests are harder to unlock even with a good interviewer.

## Setting Expectations With the Guest

- Explain the format clearly before filming: this is not an advertisement, it's a documentary-style conversation about their expertise and story. Setting this expectation up front reduces the guest defaulting to "sales pitch mode" on camera.
- Confirm what business information they want included in the end card/description (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/06_OUTRO_AND_CTA.md`).
- Get permission in advance for demonstration footage involving real customer vehicles/objects, and for showing customers or staff on camera.

## Research Before the Shoot

- Learn enough about the guest's specialty to ask informed questions, without needing to prove expertise on camera (per the Interviewer Rule, `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md` — the goal is to unlock their knowledge, not display the interviewer's).
- Prepare a guest-specific question plan drawn from `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/03_QUESTION_BANK.md` (see `04_PILOT/03_PILOT_QUESTION_PLAN.md` as a template).
- Walk the location in advance (in person or via call/photos) to plan shots — see `04_PILOT/04_PILOT_SHOT_LIST.md` as a starting checklist.

## Logistics Checklist

- [ ] Shoot date/time confirmed with guest and location
- [ ] Equipment checked and packed (see `03_AUDIO.md`, `04_CAMERA.md`)
- [ ] Release/consent paperwork prepared (guest, any visible customers/staff)
- [ ] Question plan prepared
- [ ] Shot list prepared
- [ ] Highlight-logging role assigned for the shoot day (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md`)
