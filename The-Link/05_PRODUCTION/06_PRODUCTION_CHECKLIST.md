# Production Checklist

A consolidated shoot-day checklist pulling from the rest of `05_PRODUCTION/`. Treat this as the quick-reference version; the individual documents hold the reasoning behind each item.

## Before the Shoot (`01_PRE_PRODUCTION.md`)

- [ ] Guest sourced, vetted, and briefed on the format (not an advertisement)
- [ ] Shoot date/time/location confirmed
- [ ] Guest-specific question plan prepared from `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/03_QUESTION_BANK.md`
- [ ] Shot list prepared from `04_PILOT/04_PILOT_SHOT_LIST.md` (as template)
- [ ] Consent/release paperwork prepared
- [ ] Equipment checked, charged, and packed
- [ ] Highlight-logging role assigned for shoot day

## During the Shoot (`02_FILMING.md`, `03_AUDIO.md`, `04_CAMERA.md`, `05_LOCATION.md`)

- [ ] Establishing shots of location captured
- [ ] Guest audio recorded and backed up
- [ ] Room tone/ambient sound captured
- [ ] Interview conducted using the Interviewer Rule (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`)
- [ ] Location "walk-through" footage captured (Section D of the Episode Structure)
- [ ] Demonstration footage captured, with permission
- [ ] Highlight log kept live throughout
- [ ] Human/light moments captured, including during breaks

## Immediately After the Shoot

- [ ] Footage backed up in at least two locations before leaving or shortly after
- [ ] Highlight log reviewed and completed while memory is fresh
- [ ] Guest thanked and given a realistic timeline for release
- [ ] Any outstanding release/consent paperwork finalized

## Status

This checklist should be revised after the pilot (`04_PILOT/06_PILOT_REVIEW.md`) once real gaps are identified.
