# Filming

## On-Site Flow

1. Arrive early enough to scout the actual space (plans made in pre-production often need adjusting once on location).
2. Capture wide establishing shots of the location before it fills with activity/people, if possible.
3. Let the guest walk the interviewer through the space informally before formal interview coverage begins — this warms up the guest and often surfaces the best material naturally (see `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/02_INTERVIEW_FRAMEWORK.md`, Reading the Guest).
4. Run the interview using the question plan as a reservoir, not a script, following the Interviewer Rule.
5. Capture demonstration footage opportunistically as it naturally arises, rather than only as a scheduled segment — real work doesn't always wait for the camera to be ready, so stay set up to catch it.
6. Keep rolling during setup, breaks, and transitions — some of the best human/light moments happen when the guest thinks the "real" filming has paused.

## Maintain the Highlight Log Live

A crew member (not the interviewer) should log a rough timestamp and tag whenever a highlight-category moment happens, per `03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/04_HIGHLIGHT_SYSTEM.md`. This is a filming-day responsibility, not a post-production task.

## Guest Comfort

- Keep the crew and equipment footprint as small as practical — a smaller crew generally produces a more natural, less guarded guest.
- Check in with the guest between segments; expertise interviews can be more tiring than expected for someone who isn't used to being on camera.
- Never pressure a guest into a demonstration or story they're visibly uncomfortable with, even if it would make a great clip.

## What to Avoid

- Don't over-direct the guest's language toward marketing phrases ("tell me why you're the best") — this breaks the Ad Test in `00_PROJECT_BIBLE/04_DECISION_PRINCIPLES.md` and produces unusable, promotional-sounding footage.
- Don't sacrifice the location walk-through for more sit-down interview time — the place is part of the story (`03_EPISODE_SYSTEM/01_EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md`, Section D).
