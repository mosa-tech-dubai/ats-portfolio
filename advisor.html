// Walk Away

What kills deals in this market.

None of these are visible on a test drive. All of them are findable in an afternoon.

Stop — do not buy

Flood or water-damaged car re-registered as clean

Silt lines under the carpet and inside the spare wheel well, corrosion on connector pins, and modules with impossible fault histories. Electrical faults surface months later, one control unit at a time, and never stop.

Stop — do not buy

Cut-and-shut or repaired structural damage

Fresh weld and non-factory sealer on rails and pillars, mismatched panel gaps, and a car that never tracks straight. Nothing about the geometry can be trusted again, and no ADAS or steering calibration will settle properly.

Stop — do not buy

Outstanding bank finance on the mulkiya

The transfer will not complete and the seller's "we'll settle it after" is how deposits disappear. Settlement letter first, transfer second, money last.

Price it, don't panic

Odometer inconsistent with the service record

A wound-back cluster shows up as a mismatch between module-stored mileage, service stamps and RTA test history. It is provable, and it is a negotiating lever if the car is otherwise sound.

Price it, don't panic

Non-GCC import with no cooling upgrade

US and Japan imports arrive with radiators, A/C capacity and rubber specified for a different climate. They can work here, but they need the cooling system checked properly and they always resell for less — both belong in the price.

Price it, don't panic

Recently cleared fault memory

An adaptation counter reset days before the viewing, with every module reporting zero history, tells you more than a fault code would. We read readiness and adaptation data, not just the current codes.
